## Base Weapon Statistics (Tier I)

*Weapon (scythe), unique (requires attunement)*

Forged from the crystallized blood of Lady Ovidia Morvelle, this finely balanced scythe features a razor-thin crimson blade that drinks in ambient noise. Crafted to match your current diminished stature, it lacks the sweeping reach of a full-sized polearm until resized.

- **Base Damage:** 1d10 slashing
- **Properties:** Finesse, Two-Handed (lacks Reach while in its shrunk state)
- **Magic Bonus:** +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls (scales with Tier)
- **Charges & Save DC:** Holds a maximum of 2 charges (scales with Tier). Base Scythe Save DC is 13 (scales with Tier).
- **Refilling Charges:** The scythe does not regain charges automatically. Charges may be granted alongside Favor by Lady Morvelle for services, or purchased directly:
  - **In Lady Morvelle's Presence:** 1 Favor refills 2 Charges.
  - **Afar:** 1 Favor refills 1 Charge

### Inherent Traits

- **Tainted Vitality (Curse):** The childhood poison that stole Ovidia's voice and stunted her magic still courses through her crystallized blood. While attuned to this weapon, your voice is supernaturally muffled and cannot be heard beyond 30 feet. Any attempt to shout or scream emerges at an ordinary, conversational volume. Furthermore, the blood violently rejects spoken magic: whenever you cast a spell of 1st level or higher with a verbal component, you take 1d4 poison damage per level of the spell cast.
- **The Expressive Muse (Sentience):** The scythe is a sentient weapon sharing the keen mind and force of personality of its creator. Though completely mute, it conveys distinctly expressive moods through gentle tugs on your grip, soothing warmth when pleased, or a stubborn, pouting heaviness when ignored. Once per day, at the GM's discretion, the blade may eagerly guide your strike to grant advantage on an attack roll that pleases Lady Morvelle's sensibilities, or grow abruptly heavy to impose disadvantage if used in a manner that offends her.

### Core Features Unlocked by Tier

- **Tier II (Whispering Edge):** Hits with the scythe deal an extra 1d4 necrotic damage, scaling at Tiers IV (1d6), VI (1d8), and VII (2d6).
- **Tier III (Crescendo's Hush & Crimson Ink):**
  - **Crescendo's Hush:** When you hit a creature with a melee attack using the scythe, you can expend 1 charge to deal an extra 1d8 necrotic damage and leave a parasitic vibration on the target's throat. The first time the target attempts to cast a spell with a verbal component before the start of your next turn, it must succeed on a Charisma saving throw against your Scythe DC or the spell is absorbed into the blade, failing while still expending the spell slot.
  - **Bond Evolution (Crimson Ink):** Touching the blade's point to paper or smooth stone causes tiny beads of crimson liquid to sketch playful doodles, directional arrows, or brief visual notes reflecting the scythe's (or Lady Morvelle's) commentary.
- **Tier IV (Thunderous Expulsion):** Whenever the scythe absorbs a spell via Crescendo's Hush, the glass glows with volatile kinetic energy. On your next successful melee hit with the scythe within 1 minute, it expels the trapped magic, dealing an extra 1d8 thunder damage per level of the absorbed spell (1d8 for a cantrip) and clearing the stored energy.
- **Tier V (Crescendo's End & Shared Pulse):**
  - **Crescendo's End:** The bonus damage when expending a charge for Crescendo's Hush increases to 2d8 necrotic damage.
  - **Bond Evolution (Shared Pulse):** While in the same shard, Lady Morvelle faintly senses your general emotional state and direction. Resting near the scythe brings the comforting, expensive, honeyed scent of whatever flower she currently favors that day.
- **Tier VI (Resonant Expulsion & Parlor Reflection):**
  - **Resonant Expulsion:** Thunderous Expulsion upgrades to 1d10 thunder damage per spell level (1d10 for a cantrip) and violently pushes a Large or smaller target 10 feet away from you on impact.
  - **Bond Evolution (Parlor Reflection):** In quiet moments, the polished crimson glass acts like a warm window between the two of you. Catching your reflection in the flat of the blade occasionally reveals a soft, sketched glimpse of Lady Morvelle at her desk or in her conservatory, and she may leave little drawn reactions or cheerful waves on the glass when she catches you looking.
- **Tier VII (Sovereign's Finale & One Vein):**
  - **Sovereign's Finale:** Scoring a critical hit with the scythe automatically applies the throat-silencing vibration of Crescendo's End without expending a charge. Additionally, while holding the scythe, you gain immunity to thunder damage.
  - **Bond Evolution (One Vein):** Your interests, habits, and vitality are effortlessly braided together. While in the same shard, the scythe hums with the steady, reassuring rhythm of a shared heartbeat, letting Lady Morvelle naturally sense your well-being and surroundings as if she were walking right beside you. Over time, the blade's gentle guidance causes your posture and poised mannerisms to gracefully mirror hers, and whenever you are hurt or exhausted, the glass radiates a protective, soothing warmth until you are safe again.

## Reforging & Upkeep Rules

1. **Unlocking Modifications:** Paying an upgrade's Favor cost permanently checks its **Unlocked** box in your Repertoire.
2. **Changing Loadouts:** Because Lady Morvelle must personally sculpt her crystallized blood to alter the blade, toggling a structural alteration or swapping which upgrades are marked **Equipped** requires an appointment with her and costs **2 Favor per adjustment** (capped at 5 Favor if overhauling the entire weapon in a single visit).
3. **Passive Upkeep & Buyouts:** Active upgrades require charges to fuel, whereas **Passive** upgrades draw continuously on Lady Morvelle's vitality. Each equipped Passive upgrade requires an upkeep of **1 or 2 Favor per month** (or per major campaign arc) to remain active. If left unpaid, the passive goes dormant until settled. Alternatively, you may pay a Passive upgrade's **Buyout Cost** to permanently weave that benefit into the scythe with no recurring upkeep.

## Lady Morvelle's Parlor (Personal Favors)

Favor need not go solely toward sharper glass. Lady Morvelle is always delighted when her favorite associate spends hard-earned goodwill simply seeking her company.

- **A Playful Scolding & Bandages (2 Favor):** Ideal when you have bruised your pride or put the scythe in a sour mood. Ovidia taps your forehead gently with her slender fingers, sketches an exaggerated sigh, and smooths over the weapon's temperament while serving restorative tonics. (Restores the scythe's cooperative mood and clears 1 curable ailment during a rest).
- **Sorbet on the Promenade (3 Favor):** An unhurried afternoon walk to Fiscathia's finest confiserie. She insists on choosing a flavor for you and slides wickedly funny little sketches of passing nobles across the café table. (May result in varying boons).
- **A Charcoal Keepsake (3 Favor):** You sit for Lady Morvelle while she works her sketchbook. Depending on how teasing she feels that day, the finished portrait depicts you as either an impossibly dashing figure or something adorably pocket-sized.
- **Tea & "Market Gossip" in the Conservatory (5 Favor):** Over steaming porcelain cups and delicate pastries, you hold a cozy meeting about current troubles. Between offering sugar cubes, she sketches out remarkably sharp intelligence gathered through her trade connections and the Verus Consortium.
- **A Tailored Fitting (5 Favor):** Lady Morvelle decides your current wardrobe requires immediate intervention. Her private tailors provide an immaculate outfit fitted to your exact stature, complete with discreet adventuring reinforcement and a subtle House Morvelle ensigned ribbon that signals her personal favor to polite society.
- **A Wax-Sealed Care Parcel (5 Favor):** A courier tracks you down with a ribbon-tied box containing candied fruits, remarkably soothing honey-herbal throat lozenges (a slightly teasing inclusion given the scythe's curse), and an encouraging doodle from Ovidia.
- **A Whispered Yearning (??? Favor):** You hold the crimson glass against your chest in a quiet moment and pour a specific, unspoken longing into the blade. Across the shard, Lady Morvelle feels the shape of what you wish for, answering in her own way before long.
- **The Private Opera Box (10 Favor | Req: Tier III+):** You accompany Lady Morvelle as her personal guest for the evening. Being mute, she pays far less attention to the stage than she does to passing playful notes to you behind her velvet curtain, all while making sure every present house in Fiscathia sees exactly whose side you occupy.
